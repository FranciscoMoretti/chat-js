## Start building

You get a working foundation with authentication, saved conversations, and streaming responses.

Built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, and AI SDK. Powered by [EVE](./core/architecture) for durable conversations, tool execution, and stream recovery.

```bash
npx @chat-js/cli@latest create my-app
```

Choose your [AI gateway](./gateways/overview), authentication providers, and optional capabilities during setup. The CLI assembles your app and lists the credentials your selections require.

## Make it yours

Installed tools and integrations live as editable source in your project. Change their behavior, customize the interface, or build your own tools for your product.

Use [configuration](./core/configuration) to adjust branding, models, and app settings. Follow the [tool authoring guide](./tools/authoring) to add your own capabilities, or explore the [registry](./registry) to learn how source is distributed and installed.

Deploy your app [on the web](./platforms/web), or add [desktop support](./platforms/desktop) to package it for macOS, Windows, and Linux.
